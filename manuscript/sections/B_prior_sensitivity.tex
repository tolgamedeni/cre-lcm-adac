\section{Prior sensitivity of the application fits}\label{app:prior_sens}
Table~\ref{tab:prior_sens} compares the application fits under the priors of
Section~\ref{sec:estimation} (the primary specification) with a refit under wider priors. When
almost every configuration gives the same label, the likelihood is nearly flat in the scale of the
latent effects (quasi-complete separation): larger intercepts and random-effect standard deviations
fit the near-unanimous counts almost equally well, so their absolute values, and with them the
absolute variance shares, are set by the prior. Under the wide priors the intercepts and
$\sigma_\theta$ grow, the chains mix poorly and the item share rises at the expense of the run
share. The quantities used in Section~\ref{sec:app_results} do not depend on this choice: the
ranking of the shares, the prevalence, the accuracy and Brier score of the posterior labels
against the CAP codes, the per-configuration sensitivities and specificities (which differ by at
most 0.025 and 0.005) and the posterior predictive distribution of the agreement counts.
